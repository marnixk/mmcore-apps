VOID RUNNER (mmcore edition) - asset list
=========================================================
Art direction: warm purple / crimson deep-space pixel art. Ship "Arrow" (white/red hull,
cyan canopy, orange flame), asteroid pillars with cratered caps, fixed laser gates (two
steel emitters, crimson beams running to the screen edge), pink crystals (GEMS) with a
soft glow. Hard-edged pixels, dithering, no antialiasing.

Art canvas = MODE 20 pixels; scale per resolution:
  MODE 19 1366x768  art x2 (683x384)     MODE 20 683x384   art x1 (683x384)
  1920x1080 (16)    art x3 (640x360)     1280x720 (11)     art x2 (640x360)
The game fills the whole screen at every resolution (no borders).

gfx/  (PNG, RGB; pure black = transparent for BLIT ori 4 and SPRITE READ)
  sheet_WxH.png    sprite sheet: ship poses (idle 2, thrust 2, fall, dive, hit, icon),
                   explosion (4 frames), small rocks, crystal (4) + glow (2), pillar caps
                   up/down + pillar body tile, laser emitters top/bottom + 3 beam tiles,
                   pause dither tile, small font (10 colours) and big font (4 colours)
  play_WxH.png     static gameplay backdrop (nebula, stars, ringed planet, moon, rocks)
  intro_WxH.png    title screen (VOID RUNNER logo, THREAD THE VOID ribbon, demo scene);
                   the menu panel is drawn at run time
  scores_WxH.png   high-score screen background (title + PRESS ENTER); the table is drawn
                   at run time
                   WxH = 1366x768 683x384 1920x1080 1280x720

includes/layouts.inc  GENERATED: per-resolution scale, menu/score panel positions, sheet
                      coordinates (SP_*), font tables, palette constants

Sound: none. Play is silent by design (JUKE can play in the background); music.inc holds
the switched-off hooks.

High scores: voidrunner.hi next to the program (score,gems,difficulty,name; top 10).
