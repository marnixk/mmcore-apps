Snake (mmcore edition) - asset list
=========================================================
Art direction: modern pixel art - a green garden snake with yellow diamond markings on
checkered grass, grey stone-brick walls with moss and cracks, eight fruits (apple, cherry,
banana, grapes, strawberry, orange, pear, melon) each sitting on a pulsing bright patch so
it is easy to spot. Hard-edged pixels, dithering, no antialiasing, alpha 0/255 only.

Playfield: 42 x 23 cells plus one HUD row on top, at every resolution:
  MODE 19 1366x768  cell 32 (art x2)    MODE 20 683x384   cell 16 (art x1)
  1920x1080 (16)    cell 45 (art x3)    1280x720 (11)     cell 30 (art x2)
The few pixels left over at the sides are filled with a partial stone wall.

gfx/  (PNG; alpha 0 = skipped by LOAD PNG; no sprite pixel is pure black, so BLIT ori 4
       transparency works from a CLS-black sheet page)
  sheet_WxH.png    sprite sheet, 16 cells per row:
                    0-3 head R D L U, 4-7 stone head (crash / lost life) R D L U,
                    8 body horizontal, 9 body vertical, 10-13 corners LU UR RD DL,
                    14-17 tail (tip pointing) R D L U, 18 life icon, 19-20 fruit glow
                    (2 pulse frames), 21-28 fruits, 29-32 wall variants, 33-36 floor
                    (2 tones x plain/tuft), 37-44 floor with wall shadow (T L TL corner)
                    for both tones, 45 dither (pause dim), 46-49 tongue (unused in play)
                   then the small font (8 colour rows) and the big font (4 colour rows)
  intro_WxH.png    title screen (SNAKE logo, MMCORE EDITION ribbon, fruit parade); the
                   menu panel is drawn at run time
  scores_WxH.png   high-score screen background (title + PRESS ENTER); the table is drawn
                   at run time
                   WxH = 1366x768 683x384 1920x1080 1280x720

includes/layouts.inc  GENERATED: per resolution cell size, offsets, sheet rows, panels
includes/levels.inc   GENERATED: the 10 level layouts (start position + wall pieces)

No sound at all (includes/music.inc: MUSIC_ON = 0, SFX_ON = 0) so it can run alongside
JUKE; mmcore has one global audio player and any PLAY command would stop it.

snake.hi           created on the first high score (score,level,difficulty,name per line,
                   top 10). Not shipped.

All gfx and the two generated includes come from the art generator (Python + Pillow,
kept out of the app folder); each resolution has its own pre-scaled set.
